I am an undergraduate student in Computer Science and Technology at Tsinghua University. My academic interests lie in artificial intelligence, with a particular focus on **embodied intelligence**, **robot learning**, **video generation**, and **world models**.

I am currently exploring how intelligent agents can understand, predict, and interact with the physical world. More concretely, I am interested in learning-based robotics, imitation learning and reinforcement learning, vision-language-action models, and generative models for physical reasoning.

I am also interested in the systems side of AI, including model deployment, inference acceleration, and the practical engineering required to move algorithms from papers to working systems.

My recent work and study involve embodied AI, robotic manipulation, simulation-based training, world-model-based video generation, and AI-assisted academic writing. I enjoy reading research papers, reproducing methods, building small but complete systems, and understanding not only what a model does, but why it works and where its assumptions may fail.

I am broadly motivated by a long-term question: **how can we build AI systems that do not merely generate text or images, but can form useful representations of the physical world and act reliably within it?**

**Research interests:** embodied intelligence, robot learning, robotic manipulation, imitation learning, reinforcement learning, vision-language-action models, video generation, world models, physical reasoning, model deployment, and inference acceleration.
