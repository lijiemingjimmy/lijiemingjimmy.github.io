World models are appealing because they ask a model to predict more than pixels: useful future representations,
object persistence, action consequences, and physically plausible transitions.

I am interested in video generation and predictive modeling as tools for physical reasoning. The long-term question is
whether generative models can support agents that plan and act in the world, rather than merely produce visually
convincing samples.

Topics I want to explore further:

- learning dynamics from video;
- action-conditioned prediction;
- planning with latent world models;
- evaluating physical consistency rather than only visual quality.
