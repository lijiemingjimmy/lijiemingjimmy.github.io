I enjoy reading research papers, reproducing methods, and building small but complete systems.
For me, implementation is not only a way to check whether a method works; it is also a way to understand why it works,
which assumptions it depends on, and where it may fail.

This direction includes:

- model deployment and inference acceleration;
- practical engineering around demos and prototypes;
- reproducing methods from papers;
- connecting algorithmic ideas to usable systems.
